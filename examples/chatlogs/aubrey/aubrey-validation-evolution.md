## Cycle 2: Core Hypotheses & Research Questions Refinement

### AI Critique
The Black Hat critique raised several concerns:
1. 🚨 **Testability:** Issues identified with H4 and H7.
2. ⚠️ **Missing mechanisms:** Gaps noted in H3 and H8.
3. ⚠️ **Narrow hypotheses:** H5 considered too narrow.
4. 🚨 **Theory mismatch:** H6 misaligned with expected theoretical framing.

### User Response
> Agent doesn't intervene proactively.
> Students are incentivized by their own learning, not altruism.
> [Reframed H4/H7 around search time and imposter syndrome.]

### Document Delta
| Field | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **H4/H7** | Original testability-limited framing | Reframed to specifically target search time and imposter syndrome |
| **H5** | Assumed proactive agent intervention | Corrected to reflect that the agent does *not* intervene proactively |
| **H6** | Framed around altruistic motivations | Shifted to focus on students being incentivized by their own learning |

### What shifted
- **User corrected AI framing:** Clarified that the agent is not proactive and that user motivation stems from self-learning rather than altruism.
- **User narrowed/reframed scope:** Refined abstract metrics into concrete psychological and practical barriers (search time, imposter syndrome).

## Cycle 3: Clarifying Learner-Sourcing and Critical Gaps

### User Response
> The user clarified that students do not actively write out solutions, but simply ask conceptual questions to the agent, which then implicitly updates the guide (H5). They also defined "critical gaps" as the most commonly missed topics or extensions of current subgoals (H3).

### Document Delta
| Field | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **H1 & H2** | Original framing | Reframed as true baseline ecosystem questions (Baseline Navigation, Attribution of Struggle) |
| **H3** | Ambiguous gap definition | Updated to explicitly define "critical gaps" (most commonly missed topics or extensions of current subgoals) |
| **H5** | Active student authoring | Reframed to passive conversational inference (Implicit Learner-Sourcing) |

### What shifted
- User corrected AI framing regarding the mechanism of guide updating (implicit via questions vs. explicit authoring).
- User defined thresholds for success metrics (what makes a gap "critical").
